Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the AuraGlow AI app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Ceren Dokmener (collectively, the "Application"). Ceren Dokmener is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The total time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system you use
  • Device model, app version, language, and general location derived from IP address
  • In-app actions and screen names, collected through Firebase Analytics
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data, collected through Firebase Crashlytics
  • A randomly generated app-instance identifier and, for crash reports, a Crashlytics installation identifier

Camera and Image Processing

The Application requires access to your device’s camera and photo library solely to provide personal color analysis, aura evaluation, and daily outfit feedback features. The photos you capture or select are transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to our third-party AI processing partner (OpenAI) strictly to generate real-time visual and stylistic insights. Your photos are never used to train public AI models. Please note that while our AI provider does not use your data for training, transient data may be retained within the provider's short-term operational security and abuse monitoring window (up to 30 days) in compliance with security guidelines. Photos and the content of your answers are not sent to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.


Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

The Application uses Google Firebase to understand how the Application is used and to diagnose crashes. These tools are not used to sell your personal information or to show personalized ads.

Firebase Analytics collects product-usage information such as the screens you open, in-app events (for example, starting a scan or viewing a subscription screen), session duration, app version, device model, operating system, language, and a randomly generated app-instance identifier. Event parameters are limited to feature names and short labels. We do not send your photos, quiz answers, email address, or other content you create. Advertising storage, use of data for advertising, and ad personalization are turned off. The Application does not use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and stability information in release versions of the Application, including stack traces, app version, device model, operating system version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier, so we can find and fix errors. Photos and quiz answers are not attached to crash reports.

Google processes this information as our service provider. See Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Privacy and Security in Firebase.


Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use SDKs, identifiers, and similar technologies to support core functionality, subscription delivery, product analytics, and crash diagnostics. Firebase Analytics uses an app-instance identifier for usage measurement. The Application does not use these technologies for cross-app advertising or ad personalization.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains data based on necessity:

  • User Provided Data: Retained during your active use of the Application plus 12 months thereafter, unless longer retention is required by law.
  • Automatically Collected Data & IP Logs: Retained for up to 24 months.
  • Firebase Analytics: Event-level data is retained by Google for 2 months by default, or up to 14 months if that setting is changed in the Firebase console. Aggregated statistics that do not identify you may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reports are retained for 90 days.
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: Retained indefinitely as it cannot identify any individual.
  • Temporary Image Data: Processed transiently and not retained beyond our AI provider's short-term operational and abuse-monitoring security window.
