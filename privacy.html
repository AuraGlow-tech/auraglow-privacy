Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the operator of AuraGlow AI (the “Service Provider”) processes information through the AuraGlow AI mobile application (the “Application”). You can contact the Service Provider at auraglowsup@gmail.com.

Information We Process

  • Photos you take or select for aura analysis and outfit feedback, and information you enter in the quiz or other AI features.
  • AI results and photos saved locally on your device, including your onboarding portrait and outfit check photos.
  • Limited app usage information, such as screens viewed, feature events, session information, app version, device model, operating system, language and an app-instance identifier, through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash and diagnostic information, including stack traces, device and app information and a Crashlytics installation identifier, through Firebase Crashlytics when crash reporting is enabled.
  • Subscription status, products, transaction or receipt information and identifiers needed to manage purchases through RevenueCat.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses may be processed by our service providers when the Application communicates with their services. We do not use your device’s precise location for these features.

Photos, Quiz Answers and AI Processing

With your permission, the Application accesses the camera or a photo you select to provide aura analysis and outfit feedback. Your onboarding portrait is saved in the Application’s local storage on your device; outfit check photos are also saved locally. Quiz answers and AI readings are stored locally on your device. These local files and values are not uploaded to Supabase Storage or stored in a Supabase database.

When you request an AI feature, the information needed for that request, which may include a photo and relevant answers or text, is sent over an encrypted connection to our Supabase Edge Function and then to OpenAI for analysis. The function does not intentionally save submitted photos to a file, storage bucket or database. Supabase may process request metadata and operational logs; an error log may include an OpenAI error message or model output, but our function does not intentionally log the submitted image or request body. Please avoid including sensitive information in text you submit.

OpenAI API inputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the Service Provider explicitly opts in to data sharing. The Application uses OpenAI’s Chat Completions API without requesting stored completions. OpenAI may retain API data in abuse monitoring logs for up to 30 days, subject to its applicable policies and settings. See OpenAI’s API data controls.

Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand feature use and improve the Application. Events may include whether a photo came from the camera or gallery, a result’s archetype category key, a selected life domain, a paywall placement, a plan type and a short purchase outcome label. We do not intentionally send photos, image URLs, quiz answer text, AI-generated aura text or email addresses as event parameters. Analytics also uses an app-instance identifier and may automatically collect device and usage information.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and stability information to diagnose errors in release builds. We do not intentionally attach photos or quiz answers, set a Crashlytics user identifier, or add custom keys or logs. Crash reports can nevertheless include exception messages and diagnostic context generated when an error occurs. When Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are used together, crash reports may include recent analytics event breadcrumbs.

We have disabled advertising storage and personalization in the Application’s analytics configuration. We do not use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) or these services for cross-app advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and Purchases

RevenueCat processes subscription and purchase information, such as product identifiers, subscription status, transaction and receipt information, app user identifiers and limited device information, to validate purchases and provide access to paid features. Apple or Google processes payment card details; we do not collect or store your payment card number.

Service Providers and Sharing

We share information needed to operate the Application with the following providers:

  • Supabase: operates the Edge Function that relays AI requests and serves curated images from our storage buckets. User photos are not written to those buckets.
  • OpenAI: processes submitted photos and relevant inputs to produce AI analysis.
  • RevenueCat: manages in-app purchases and subscription entitlements.
  • Google Firebase: provides usage analytics and crash diagnostics.

We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security and integrity of users and the Application. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Storage, Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data: Your locally saved photos, quiz answers and readings remain on your device until you remove them through available app or device controls, clear app data, or uninstall the Application. Uninstalling does not delete information already processed by external providers.
  • AI requests: We do not intentionally keep submitted photos in Supabase Storage or a database. OpenAI may retain API data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring under its applicable policies. Operational logs, including possible error messages or model output, are subject to Supabase’s configured log retention.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: Firebase data is retained under the applicable project settings and Google’s retention policies. Crashlytics generally retains crash data for 90 days before beginning removal from active and backup systems.
  • Purchases: Purchase and transaction records may remain with RevenueCat and the applicable app store for subscription administration and legal or accounting obligations.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of data we can identify and control, email auraglowsup@gmail.com. We may need information to verify your request. Some provider-held analytics and diagnostic records cannot be matched to a person from an email address alone. We will explain what we can delete and what must be retained for legal or transaction purposes. If the Application later offers account creation, account deletion controls will be provided in the Application as required by the applicable app store rules.

Your Choices

You can decline camera or photo access and avoid using AI features that require an image. You can remove the Application to stop future collection from your device. If a choice or withdrawal of consent is available under applicable law, contact us at the address below; withdrawal does not undo processing already completed.

International Processing and Security

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, applicable legal safeguards govern these transfers. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted transport for AI requests, to protect data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age in their region. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Application or our data practices. The effective date below will be updated when the policy changes.
