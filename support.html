Subscriptions & Purchases

AuraGlow AI offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. You can view, manage or cancel your subscription through Apple's subscription settings .

If you previously purchased a subscription and your Premium access is not showing, use the Restore Purchases option inside AuraGlow AI.

Refund requests for purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple and can be submitted through reportaproblem.apple.com .

Aura Readings

If you're having trouble completing your Aura reading, uploading a photo, or viewing your results, make sure you have an active internet connection and try restarting the Application.
